# Quick Start Guide: Real-Time QR Generator

## How to Use the New Real-Time QR Generator

### 1. Access the Generator
- Log in to your dashboard
- Click "Generate QR Code" from the menu or dashboard

### 2. Create Your QR Code

#### Step 1: Choose QR Code Type
Select from 15+ types in the dropdown:
- **URL** - Website links
- **vCard** - Business cards / Contact info
- **Text** - Plain text messages
- **Email** - Pre-filled email
- **SMS** - Text messages
- **Phone** - Direct phone calls
- **WiFi** - Network credentials
- **Social Media** - Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube
- **App Stores** - iOS App Store, Google Play
- **Events** - Calendar events
- **Location** - GPS coordinates / Maps

#### Step 2: Fill in Information
- Form updates automatically based on selected type
- Required fields are marked
- Helpful hints appear below fields

#### Step 3: Watch the Preview
- **Preview appears automatically** as you type (after 800ms)
- Left side: Your form
- Right side: Live QR code preview
- No need to click anything!

#### Step 4: Customize (Optional)
Scroll down to customize:
- **Colors**: Foreground and background
- **Size**: Width/height in pixels
- **Error Correction**: L, M, Q, or H
- **Border**: White space around QR code
- **Styles**: Square, rounded, dots, etc.
- **Advanced**: Gradients, frames, eye styles

All changes update the preview in real-time!

#### Step 5: Save & Download
- Click "Generate QR Code" button at bottom
- QR code saves to your account
- Download in your chosen format (PNG, SVG, PDF)

### 3. Quick Actions

In the preview panel:
- **Quick View** - Temporary zoom for inspection
- **Refresh Preview** - Manually regenerate if needed

### 4. Tips for Best Results

✅ **DO:**
- Use clear, concise information
- Choose appropriate error correction for your use case
- Test QR codes after generation
- Use high contrast colors for better scanning
- Keep text content under 2000 characters

❌ **AVOID:**
- Very long URLs (use URL shorteners)
- Low contrast color combinations
- Too much border (wastes space)
- Complex gradients for outdoor use

## Troubleshooting

### Preview Not Showing?
- Check that you've filled required fields
- Wait 1 second after typing
- Click "Refresh Preview" button
- Check browser console for errors

### QR Code Not Scanning?
- Increase error correction level
- Use higher contrast colors
- Make QR code larger
- Reduce border size if too small
- Test with multiple QR scanner apps

### Colors Not Working?
- Use hex color format (#000000)
- Ensure sufficient contrast
- Avoid transparent colors
- Check dark mode compatibility

### Slow Performance?
- Reduce QR code size temporarily
- Disable gradients if not needed
- Close other browser tabs
- Check internet connection
